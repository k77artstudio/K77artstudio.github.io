I.

How resale works

K77 does not simply sell art and disappear. When a collector is ready to let a piece move on, the studio facilitates the transition end to end — sourcing the next collector, verifying the work, and updating the piece's permanent record.

Eligibility

A piece becomes eligible for studio-facilitated resale after it has been held for two years. This applies to every K77 edition without exception — enough time for the piece's story, and the studio's, to deepen before it changes hands.

Resale is never required. Two years marks when it becomes possible, not when it's expected — many collectors keep their pieces for life, and the piece's provenance record grows richer the longer it stays with one owner.

Notify the studio

The collector reaches out to K77 directly. The piece's Collector ID and provenance history are pulled and verified.

Valuation

K77 assesses current fair value — based on the original price, time held, collector demand, and the piece's condition — and proposes an asking price to the seller.

Sourcing the buyer

K77 draws on its collector waitlist and network to find the next owner, so the seller never has to find a buyer alone. The studio actively sources for an initial window of three months.

If no buyer has been found within that window, K77 and the seller revisit together — either adjusting the asking price, or keeping the piece listed on the waitlist with no forced timeline. The choice stays with the seller.

Transfer & record

On sale, the K77 Chain provenance registry is updated, the Collector ID transfers to the new owner, and the seller is paid out.

Transfer outside a sale

A piece can also change hands as a gift or inheritance, without going through resale. In these cases, simply notify the studio so the Collector ID and provenance record can be updated to reflect the new owner.

II.

Delivery & damage guarantee

Every K77 piece is covered by a one-time reproduction guarantee for two years from the date of delivery. If the piece is damaged or broken during this period — whether in transit or afterward, in the collector's care — K77 will reproduce the piece once at no cost for the artwork itself. Delivery of the reproduction is the collector's responsibility.

This is a separate two-year period from resale eligibility below — one covers the piece's condition, the other covers when it can change hands. Both begin at delivery, but they run independently.

III.

On value, honestly

K77 does not promise a fixed return — no serious collector's market does. What K77 offers instead is a studio actively managing scarcity, provenance, and demand on the collector's behalf, so value has room to grow rather than being left to chance.

As a general guide, and market conditions permitting, pieces resold after the two-year eligibility mark are expected to trend toward 10–20% above their original price. This is a guide, not a guarantee, and will sharpen as more of the collection changes hands.

IV.

Why this, and not a certificate on paper

A paper certificate can be lost, forged, or forgotten. The K77 Chain is a living record — every piece's history lives digitally, tied to a Collector ID that updates with each transfer, viewable at any time.

Where a digital token trades speculatively and can be copied endlessly, a K77 piece is one physical object with one verified history. The scarcity is real. The record is permanent. The studio does not disappear after the sale.
